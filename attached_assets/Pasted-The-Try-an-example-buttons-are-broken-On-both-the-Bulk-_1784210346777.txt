The "Try an example" buttons are broken. On both the Bulk Compare and Link Gap Finder pages, clicking "Try an example" does nothing at all — no fields populate and no sample results load. This happened consistently on repeated attempts.
Several background/progressive-loading features appear to stall indefinitely. A brand-new domain lookup's "Historical Trend" chart said it was "building" and would fill in "usually within a minute or two," but stayed stuck at a single data point the whole time I watched. Similarly, the Corpus Audit tool for wikipedia.org got stuck at "2 of 4 crawls fetched" for 30+ seconds with no progress, and sub-sections like "Live robots.txt check pending…," "robots.txt Time Machine," and "Fetching the live sitemap…" never resolved. These may just be slow, but nothing progressed during my testing, which suggests they may hang rather than complete.
The Link Gap Finder table is silently truncated. It reports 25,000 total gaps and 2,778 "attainable" ones, but only ever renders the top 500 rows via the API, and every one of those 500 rows is labeled "ATTAINABLE" — with no pagination control, "load more" button, or "showing 500 of 25,000" indicator anywhere on the page. This makes the attainable count hard to reconcile with what's actually shown and could mislead users about coverage.
Subdomain input is silently normalized without a clear callout. Searching "news.ycombinator.com" quietly returns data for the root domain "ycombinator.com" instead, with only a small, easy-to-miss "Subdomains not scanned yet" note — someone could easily think they're looking at subdomain-specific authority data when they're not.
Newly-looked-up domains show a lot of "not available yet" placeholders (referring domains, spam/toxicity flags, change tracking) since that data only populates after a monthly sync — this is disclosed in the methodology, so it's more of a limitation than a bug, but worth knowing since most first-time lookups on less-popular domains will hit this.
